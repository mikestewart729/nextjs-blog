# An “Approachable” Introduction to My Thesis Research

## Overview

My thesis is titled “Dynamics of matter wave quantum emitters in engineered reservoirs”, which in addition to being a mouthful, is jargon heavy and unhelpful. I can imagine your eyes glazing over as you read it! Let me do my best to illuminate what each of these terms mean, and hopefully teach you something fascinating about the universe in the process. 

Please note that I will endeavor for this blog to be approachable, so at times I may sacrifice true rigor. If you know physics, I hope you’ll forgive me this lapse in the interest of science communication for a broad audience.

In this, and the following several series of blog posts, I will do my best to introduce you to the fascinating world of matter waves and engineered reservoirs!
